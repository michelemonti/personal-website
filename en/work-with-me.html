When it works, when it doesn't

It makes sense to work together if

  • You have a concrete production, product or process problem — not a marketing problem.
  • You want someone who has seen machines, materials and code, not just slides.
  • You want reasoned decisions, even when the answer is "don't do it".
  • You're willing to involve the people who do the work, not just those who talk about it.

Probably not if

  • You're looking for a full-time body for hire, indefinitely.
  • The goal is "doing something with AI" without a problem to solve.
